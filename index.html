About these fares

Rates are the official BRTA fares from the gazette notification of 23 September 2026 (effective 22 September 2026) for diesel-run buses and minibuses. Gas-run buses are not covered. Fares are rounded to the nearest whole taka and the minimum fare applies to short trips. Actual fares can differ if an operator charges a different rate — check the fare chart in the bus.

Diesel-run buses only — gas-run buses are not covered by these rates.
